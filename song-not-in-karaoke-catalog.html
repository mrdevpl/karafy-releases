Song not in your karaoke catalog? Make it yourself

Karaoke catalogs cover the big hits. The song your guest asks for, a local band, a track in your own language: often it simply isn't there. Here is how to make the karaoke version yourself.

Why songs go missing

Catalog services license songs one by one and re-record many of them. That costs money, so they focus on what most people sing. Lesser-known artists, new releases and songs outside the English-language charts are the first to be left out.

If you already have the song as an audio file, you don't have to wait for a catalog to add it.

What a karaoke track needs

  • A backing track — the song with the lead vocals taken out.
  • The lyrics, split into lines that fit on screen.
  • Timing — when each line and each word starts, so the highlight follows the singer.

Doing this by hand takes hours per song. Karafy does all three automatically and leaves you a few minutes of checking.

Step by step with Karafy

  1. Open the song file (MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG, MP4 or MKV) and click Generate.
  2. Karafy separates the vocals, transcribes the lyrics with the timing of every word and checks them against published lyrics.
  3. Open the built-in editor and fix anything that is off. How much needs fixing depends on the song: a clear vocal over a simple arrangement often needs nothing, a dense mix or a heavy accent needs more.
  4. Export in the format your setup uses: KFN for KaraFun-compatible players, MP3+G for karaoke hardware, MP4 for any screen, or LRC for apps that show synced lyrics.

Songs in other languages

Karafy transcribes many languages, not only English — enter the song's language code (for example es or pl) before you generate for the best result. This is where your own tracks matter most: catalogs are thinnest outside English.
