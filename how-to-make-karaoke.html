How to make karaoke from a song on your PC

A step-by-step guide to turning a song you already have into a karaoke track with synced lyrics — no studio skills, no subscription.

What you need

  • A song as an audio or video file — MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG, MP4 or MKV — that you have the right to use.
  • A computer with Windows 10 or 11. No graphics card required.
  • Karafy — free for your first 10 songs.

Step by step

  1. Open the song. Start Karafy and pick your audio or video file. That is the only thing you have to do by hand — everything below runs in one go.
  2. Vocals are separated. Karafy splits the track into the singer's voice and a clean instrumental. The instrumental becomes your backing track; the isolated voice makes the next step far more accurate.
  3. Lyrics are transcribed. Speech-recognition AI listens to the vocals and writes down every word with its exact timing. It runs on your own computer, so in local mode nothing is uploaded.
  4. Lyrics are checked and synced. The transcript is compared with published lyrics to fix misheard words, then every word is aligned to the music.
  5. Check it in the editor. Play the result, fix a line or nudge a timestamp if something is off. Most songs need little or nothing.
  6. Export. Save as KFN, MP4, MP3+G or LRC — see the table below.

Which format to choose

FormatBest for
KFNKaraFun-compatible karaoke players. Karafy creates two files: one with the original track, one with the clean backing.
MP4Any video player, a TV, or a laptop at a party.
MP3+GKaraoke machines that read CD+G.
LRCMusic apps that show synced lyrics.

Tips for the best result

  • Use the studio version of a song rather than a live recording — clearer vocals mean better lyrics.
  • Pick a good-quality file; heavily compressed audio makes vocal separation harder.
  • Songs with heavy effects on the voice may need a few fixes in the editor.
  • On a slow computer, cloud mode does the work on our servers and uses larger AI models.
