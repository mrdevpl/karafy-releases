For karaoke hosts: play the requests your catalog doesn't have

Every host knows the moment: a guest asks for a song and the search comes back empty. Here is a simple workflow to turn those requests into karaoke tracks for your next show.

Collect the misses

Keep a running list of every request you couldn't play — a note on your phone is enough. After a few shows you will see the same titles come back: local favourites, songs in your audience's language, new releases. Those are the tracks worth making first.

Make them between shows

  1. Get the song as an audio file you have the right to use (MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG, MP4 or MKV).
  2. Add the songs to Karafy and click Generate. It separates the vocals and transcribes and times the lyrics on your own PC.
  3. Check every song in the built-in editor before it goes on stage. A wrong word in front of a room is more noticeable than at home, so give each track a quick read-through.
  4. Export in the format your show runs on.

Which format for which setup

  • MP3+G — the long-standing standard for karaoke hosting software and CD+G hardware.
  • KFN — for KaraFun-compatible players.
  • MP4 — plays on any laptop, TV or projector without karaoke software.
  • LRC — synced lyrics for apps that display them next to the music.

You can export the same song in several formats, so one track works across different venues.

Tips for show-ready tracks

  • Start from the best-quality file you have — a clean studio recording gives a cleaner backing track.
  • For songs you will play often, use cloud processing: our strongest models give a cleaner instrumental and more accurate lyrics. It runs on cloud credits, included with Pro and sold in credit packs.
  • Everything plays offline, so a weak venue Wi-Fi never stops the show.
  • Performing songs in public usually needs a licence from your local music rights organisation — check what applies where you host.
